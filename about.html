About BT Energy

Company overview

Practical electrical solutions, built around safety.

BT Energy (Pty) Ltd is a South African electrical services company focused on low-voltage electrical systems. Our work spans installations, maintenance and repairs, distribution boards and control panels, testing and commissioning, industrial electrical services, and solar and backup power solutions.

We aim to serve residential and commercial customers with dependable, practical and cost-conscious solutions, supported by clear communication and quality workmanship.

Our vision

To become a trusted South African provider of low-voltage electrical and energy solutions, recognised for dependable service, quality workmanship and a strong commitment to safety.
